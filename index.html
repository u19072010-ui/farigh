About Us

Born from a love of salt, sand and horizons.

BeautyOfBeaches is an independent discovery platform helping travellers find, compare and fall in love with the world's most famous beaches. From Iceland's volcanic shores to the Caribbean's golden sunsets, we bring accurate, inspiring information together in one place.

Our Mission

To make exploring the world's beaches simple, inspiring and accessible to everyone.

Our Vision
